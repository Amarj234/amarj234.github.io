Privacy Policy for SafeAura

Last updated: 18 September 2026

SafeAura ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") is a parental-control and content-blocking application for Android (package name safeaura.domain.blocker). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, and the choices you have.

By installing and using SafeAura, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Summary (the short version)

  • SafeAura performs its blocking on your device. Your blocklist, blocked apps and parent PIN are stored locally on the device only.
  • We (the developer) do not operate any server and do not collect, store, sell, or share your personal data, browsing history, or DNS queries.
  • The App shows advertisements through Google AdMob, which collects certain data as described below and in Google's own privacy policy.
  • The App uses a local VPN solely to filter DNS requests and block selected apps. It is not a remote/proxy VPN and does not send your traffic to us.

1. Information the App accesses

a) Data stored locally on your device

The following is created and stored only on your device (using the device's local storage / secure storage) and is never transmitted to us:

  • Blocklist – the website domains you choose to block.
  • Blocked apps – the list of apps you choose to block.
  • Parent PIN – used to protect settings and controls. It is stored locally and is not sent anywhere.
  • App preferences – such as your selected theme (light/dark) and language.

You can delete all of this data at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it.

b) Installed-app detection

To offer the "Block social apps" feature, the App checks whether specific, well-known social-media apps are installed on the device (for example Instagram, YouTube, WhatsApp). This check happens on the device, is limited to a fixed list of known package names, and the result is not transmitted to us or any third party.

c) DNS and network filtering (local VPN)

SafeAura uses Android's VpnService to run a local, on-device filter:

  • DNS requests are inspected on the device. Requests for domains on your blocklist are blocked locally (answered with a null address). Requests for allowed domains are forwarded to public DNS resolvers (Google Public DNS – 8.8.8.8 / 8.8.4.4) so normal browsing continues to work.
  • For apps you choose to hard-block, that app's network traffic is dropped on the device.

The App does not record, store, or transmit your browsing history or DNS queries to us. The local VPN does not route your web traffic through any server operated by us. Please note that DNS requests for allowed domains are handled by Google Public DNS and are subject to Google's privacy practices.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays banner and interstitial ads using Google AdMob. To serve ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as:

  • Advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / App Set ID)
  • Device information (device model, operating system, coarse/approximate location derived from IP, language)
  • Ad interaction and performance data

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's policies:

  • How Google uses information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can limit ad personalization by adjusting Settings → Google → Ads on your device (for example "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization").

3. Permissions the App requests

Permission Why it is used
VPN service (BIND_VPN_SERVICE) To run the on-device DNS/app filter that performs blocking
Foreground service To keep protection running reliably while enabled
Post notifications To show the ongoing "protection is on" notification required by Android
Internet / network state For DNS forwarding and to load advertisements
Package visibility (<queries>) To detect which of a fixed set of social apps are installed

The App does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, precise location, SMS, or call logs.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. Information is shared only in these limited ways, which are inherent to how the App works:

  • Google AdMob – for showing advertisements (see Section 2).
  • Public DNS (Google Public DNS) – allowed DNS requests are forwarded to resolve domains so browsing works.

We do not share any data with any other third party.

5. Children's privacy

SafeAura is a parental-control tool intended to be installed and configured by a parent or guardian. It is not directed at children to create accounts or submit personal information, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App does not require an account or any sign-in. Advertising is provided by Google AdMob; where the App is used on a child's device, the parent/guardian remains responsible for the device's ad and privacy settings.

6. Data retention

All data the App creates (blocklist, blocked apps, PIN, preferences) stays on your device until you remove it. Clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App permanently removes this information from the device.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Turn off blocking at any time from within the App (protected by the parent PIN).
  • Delete your data by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it.
  • Control ad personalization via your device's Google Ads settings.

8. Security

Your data remains on your device. The parent PIN is stored using the device's secure storage. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this document. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
