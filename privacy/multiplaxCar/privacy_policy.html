Privacy Policy – Multiplex Car

Author: Balliram · Contact: amarj234@gmail.com · Last updated: 8 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Multiplex Car mobile game ("the App") handles, why, and the choices you have. The App has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. We do not sell personal information.

1. Information stored only on your device

Your game progress (stars, coins, cars, paints and settings such as sound, vibration and graphics) is saved locally on your device. It is never uploaded to us and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

2. Information collected by third-party services

The App uses the following Google services, which may collect information automatically:

ServicePurposeData
Google AdMobShowing banner, native, interstitial and rewarded video ads Advertising ID, IP address (used for approximate location), device and app information, ad interactions, crash/diagnostic data
Firebase Cloud MessagingSending game notifications (new levels, rewards, events) A Firebase installation ID and push token, device information
Cloud Firestore (Firebase)Downloading app settings such as ad configuration IP address and device information as part of the network request; no personal data is written

See Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses data from partner apps and Firebase privacy information.

3. Advertising and consent

  • Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states) the App asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options inside the App.
  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Rewarded ads are always optional; you choose whether to watch one in exchange for bonus coins.

4. Notifications

On Android 13 and later the App asks for permission before sending notifications. You can turn them off at any time in your device's app settings.

5. Data sharing, transfer and security

Data collected by the services above is processed by Google LLC and may be transferred to and stored in other countries. It is transmitted using encryption (HTTPS/TLS). We do not share data with anyone else.

6. Data retention and deletion

Game data on your device is removed when you uninstall the App. Push tokens become inactive when the App is uninstalled. Data held by Google is retained according to Google's policies. To ask about or request deletion of any data associated with you, email amarj234@gmail.com.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your personal data. Contact us at the address above to exercise them.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed; significant changes will be noted in the App's store listing.
