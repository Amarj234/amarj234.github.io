Privacy Policy

Local Chat: Offline AI · Effective 5 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Local Chat: Offline AI (the "App"), published by Balliram ("we", "us"), handles information. In short: AI models run entirely on your device. Your messages, photos and documents are never uploaded, we have no servers or accounts, and the App contains no analytics, advertising or tracking. The internet is used only to browse and download AI models from Hugging Face.

1. Information stored only on your device

The following is saved in the App's private storage on your device. We never receive it:

  • Conversations: the messages you type, the AI's replies, the name of the model used and generation statistics (such as reply speed).
  • Photos and documents you attach: a copy of each photo you pick or take, and the text extracted from PDF or text files. They are processed on-device by the AI model and kept while the conversation exists.
  • Settings: theme, assistant mode, custom instructions and generation options.
  • Downloaded AI models and the list of models you have downloaded.

2. Information we collect

None. The App has no user accounts, no sign-in, no in-app purchases and no servers of our own. It does not include analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking software, and it does not collect device identifiers, location, contacts or usage statistics.

3. Hugging Face (model downloads)

The App connects to the internet only when you browse or search for models online, or download one. These requests go directly from your device, over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, to Hugging Face (huggingface.co), which hosts the model files, and to the content-delivery servers it uses. Hugging Face receives:

  • your IP address and standard technical request information (such as the time of the request and the app's network library version);
  • which models and files are listed, checked or downloaded;
  • any search words you type in the model search.

Your conversations, photos, documents and settings are never sent. Hugging Face handles this information under its own privacy policy: huggingface.co/privacy.

Chatting with a downloaded model works without any internet connection.

4. When you contact us or report a response

Reporting an AI response copies a report (the reason you chose, the model name and the text of that response) for you to send to us by email. Nothing is sent automatically. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it only to answer you and review the report, and delete it on request.

5. Permissions the App uses

  • Internet: to browse and download AI models from Hugging Face.
  • Microphone (audio): only when you tap the microphone button to dictate a message. The App uses your device's built-in speech-recognition service to turn your speech into text, and its built-in text-to-speech service to read replies aloud. Voice input can be turned off in Settings at any time.
  • Photos and camera: only when you choose to attach a picture. The App uses the system photo picker or camera and receives only the image you select or take. On iPhone, iOS asks for your permission first.
  • Files: only when you choose to attach a document, through the system file picker.

The App does not use your contacts or location.

Speech recognition may run on your device or use your device provider's online service, depending on your device and language settings. If the online service is used, your spoken audio is sent directly by your device to that provider (for example Google) under its own privacy policy. We do not record, receive or store your voice, and voice input is optional.

6. AI-generated content

Replies are produced by AI models running on your device and may be inaccurate, incomplete or inappropriate. Do not rely on them for medical, legal, financial or other professional advice. Models are created by third parties and provided under their own licences.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Data retention and security

Your data stays in the App's private storage, protected by your device's security (screen lock and storage encryption), until you delete it or uninstall the App. On Android, app backup is disabled. On iPhone, downloaded models are excluded from backup, while chats and settings may be included in your own iCloud or computer backup like other app data. Because nothing is uploaded to us, there is no server-side copy.

9. Your choices

  • Delete a chat from the chat list, or every chat with Settings → Delete all chats (this also deletes attached photos).
  • Delete downloaded models in Settings → Manage models → Downloaded.
  • Uninstall the App to remove all of its data from your device.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal information. As we hold none of your data, you control it directly in the App; for data held by Hugging Face, contact Hugging Face. You can also contact us and we will help.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, the App will show you the new version and ask you to agree before you continue.
