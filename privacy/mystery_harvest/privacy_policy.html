Privacy Policy

App: Mystery Harvest
Last Updated: October 28, 2026
Version: 1.0.0
Summary: Mystery Harvest is a completely offline-first puzzle game. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All game data is stored locally on your device only.

1. Data We Collect

Mystery Harvest does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII). The only data stored is local game progress on your device:

  • Game Progress: Level completion, stars earned, scores, moves count
  • Settings: Sound on/off, haptics on/off, selected theme
  • Statistics: Total moves, total harvests, daily streaks, best scores
  • Purchase Records: Local receipt validation for "Remove Ads" and coin purchases

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No name, email, phone number, or contact information
  • No location data or GPS coordinates
  • No device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, Advertising ID)
  • No contacts, photos, or media files
  • No microphone or camera access
  • No analytics or tracking of gameplay behavior

3. How Data Is Used

All data is used exclusively to provide and improve your gaming experience:

  • Save and restore your game progress across sessions
  • Remember your preferences (sound, haptics, theme)
  • Track your personal statistics and achievements
  • Validate purchase receipts locally for "Remove Ads" and coins
  • Track daily offline play time (stored locally only)

4. Data Storage

All game data is stored locally on your device using:

  • Flutter's shared_preferences (encrypted key-value storage)
  • No cloud sync, no external databases, no third-party servers
  • Data persists until you uninstall the app or use "Reset Progress" in Settings

5. Advertising

Mystery Harvest displays Google AdMob advertisements to support development:

  • Banner ads at bottom of game screen
  • Interstitial ads shown every 3 levels (optional)
  • Rewarded ads optional - watch for bonus coins

Google AdMob may collect device information as per their Privacy Policy. You can disable personalized ads in your device settings.

6. In-App Purchases

Mystery Harvest offers optional in-app purchases:

  • Remove Ads (₹99 / $1.99) - Non-consumable, permanent
  • Coin Packages - Consumable coin packs

Purchases are processed securely through Google Play Billing / App Store. We do not receive or store payment information. Receipts are validated locally on your device.

7. Offline Play & Data

Mystery Harvest supports offline play with a daily 10-minute limit:

  • Offline play time is tracked locally only
  • After 5 minutes: warning shown with remaining time
  • After 10 minutes: play blocked until internet reconnection
  • Reconnecting after 10+ min offline: optional rewarded ad shown
  • Daily limit resets at midnight local time

Purchasing "Remove Ads" removes the offline time limit entirely.

8. In-App Purchases & Refunds

All purchases are processed through Google Play Billing (Android) or App Store (iOS):

  • We do not handle payment data directly
  • Refunds handled per platform policy (Google Play / App Store)
  • Contact platform support for billing issues

9. Children's Privacy

Mystery Harvest is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app contains no social features, chat, or user-generated content.

10. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Data persists until you uninstall the app
  • Use "Reset Progress" in Settings → Reset Progress to delete all local data
  • Uninstalling the app removes all local data permanently
  • No server-side data to delete (no servers used)

11. Third-Party Services

Mystery Harvest uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: Advertising (see Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google Play Billing / Apple App Store: In-app purchases
  • Flutter/Google: Framework only, no data collection

No other third-party analytics, crash reporting, or tracking services are used.

12. Security

  • All local data stored in platform-encrypted storage
  • No network transmission of game data
  • Purchase validation performed locally on device
  • No API endpoints or backend servers

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Changes will be posted here with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the app's data practices, please contact us through the app store listing's "Contact Developer" link, or visit our support page.

15. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the jurisdiction in which the app is distributed (Google Play Store / Apple App Store terms apply).
