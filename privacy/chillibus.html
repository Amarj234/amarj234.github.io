Chilli Bus: Traffic Jam

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026 · Last updated: 24 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Chilli Bus: Traffic Jam (the "App"), published by Balliram ("we", "us"), handles information when you use it. You can play without an account, and we never ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts.

1. Information stored only on your device

Your game progress is saved on your own device and is not sent to us: your Easy and Hard levels, coins, boosters, bought bus paints and home themes, daily missions progress, lucky spin status and your sound and daily reminder settings. The App also keeps a copy of its ad settings on the device. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes all of this.

Optional daily reminders are scheduled and shown by your device. They do not use a push service and nothing about them is sent to us (see section 6).

2. Information processed by third-party services

The App uses services provided by Google, which may collect information automatically as described here and in Google's own privacy policies. These services start only after you agree to these terms. We do not sell your personal information.

Google AdMob (advertising): the App is free and shows native ads on some screens, full-screen ads between some levels, and optional rewarded ads. To show, measure and protect ads, AdMob may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • IP address, and an approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (ads viewed or tapped) and diagnostic information.

Ads may be personalised where the law allows and, in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, only with your consent.

Google Analytics for Firebase (usage statistics): records how the App is used, such as app opens, session length, screens, app updates and when a level is completed or failed (with the level number). It uses an app-instance identifier, and may use the advertising ID, together with device information and an approximate location derived from your IP address. We use these statistics to improve the game and to choose which in-app messages to show.

Firebase In-App Messaging: occasionally shows game messages (for example an event or reward announcement) inside the App. It uses a Firebase installation ID and the usage statistics above to decide which message to show, and records whether you viewed or tapped it.

Firebase Cloud Messaging (notifications): Firebase assigns your device a registration token and subscribes it to a general news topic so we can send occasional game notifications, such as rewards, events and reminders. A notification may contain a link (for example to the Play Store) that opens when you tap it.

Cloud Firestore (game settings): the App reads a small settings file (for example, whether ads are on). Nothing about you is stored there; Google receives your IP address and the technical data needed to answer the request.

Google Play In-App Updates: on Android the App asks Google Play whether a newer version is available, so it can download and install the update.

More: policies.google.com/privacy and firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

3. In-app purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases to buy coin packs and a "Remove Ads" upgrade. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS) — we do not receive, store or process any payment information. When you make a purchase, the store provides the App with a signed receipt so the coins or ad-free status can be unlocked on your device. Purchase history is stored locally on your device and is not sent to our servers. You can manage or cancel subscriptions (if any) and request refunds through your Google Play or Apple account settings.

4. What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise location or payment information. Payment details are handled by Google Play or the App Store; we never see them.

5. Your choices and consent

  • Ad consent: in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, Google's consent message is shown before personalised ads are used. Change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy Settings.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Notifications: allow or block them when asked, or later in your device settings. Daily reminders can also be turned off in Settings → Daily reminders.
  • Game data: Settings → Reset Progress resets your levels, coins, daily missions and reminder setting; uninstalling the App or clearing its data in your device settings deletes all game data.

6. Notifications and permissions

Daily reminders: if you allow notifications, the App shows up to five reminders a day (for example when your free spin is ready, how your daily missions are going, or which level is next), and a few "come back" reminders if you haven't played for some days. They are scheduled and shown by your device, using the progress saved on it. They are not sent through Firebase Cloud Messaging or any other push service, and nothing about them is sent to us, to Google or to anyone else. You can turn them off at any time in Settings → Daily reminders or in your device's notification settings.

Permissions the App uses:

  • Internet and network state: to load ads, game settings, messages and notifications.
  • Notifications: to show game notifications and the daily reminders (Android 13 and later asks you first).
  • Run at start-up: so reminders that are already scheduled are restored after your device restarts, and so the notification service can deliver notifications.
  • Vibration and wake lock: used by the notification service to deliver notifications and reminders.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob and Firebase as described above.

On iOS, the App asks for permission before showing any notification. The App does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, storage or location.

7. Children's privacy

The App is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Data retention and security

Game data stays on your device until you reset it or uninstall the App. Information collected by Google is kept according to Google's retention policies (for Analytics, by the retention setting of our Firebase project). Communication with Google's services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, and to object to its processing. As we keep no personal information on our own servers, most requests are handled by Google; you can also contact us and we will help.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, the App will show you the new version and ask you to agree before you continue playing.
