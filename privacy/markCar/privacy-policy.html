Mark Car – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Mark Car: Draw Bridge (package com.mark.car, “the app”) handles. “We” means the developer of the app.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (unlocked levels, stars, daily streak, daily goals progress and free hints) and settings (sound, daily reminders on or off) are stored only on your device. We do not run servers, have no user accounts and do not receive this data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Notifications and permissions

Daily reminders. If you allow notifications, the app shows up to five reminders a day (for example to keep your streak going, how your daily goals are going, or which level is next), and a few “come back” reminders if you haven’t played for some days. They are scheduled and shown by your device, using the progress saved on it. No push notification service is used, and nothing about the reminders is sent to us, to Google or to anyone else. You can turn them off at any time in Settings › Daily reminders in the app or in Android settings.

Android permissions. The app requests:

  • Internet and network-state access, which ads need
  • Notifications (Android 13 and later ask you first), to show the daily reminders
  • Run at startup, so the reminders that are already scheduled are restored after your device restarts
  • Vibration, used by the reminders
  • The advertising ID and related ad permissions the Google Mobile Ads SDK adds

The app does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or precise location.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob (banner, native, interstitial and optional rewarded video ads). To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, approximate location derived from IP, and ad interactions. Google’s use of this data is described in its policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google’s User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice at any time from Settings › Privacy & ad choices in the app. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads).

In-app purchases

The app offers optional in-app purchases (for example, a one-time “Remove Ads” upgrade or hint packs). Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive your payment details—Google handles the transaction and provides the app with a purchase token so it can unlock the feature. Purchase history is linked to your Google account and governed by Google’s policies. You can view, manage or request refunds for purchases in the Play Store app (Profile ▸ Payments & subscriptions ▸ Budget & history).

Children

The app is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal data. Apart from the ad processing by Google described above, no data leaves your device.

Security and retention

Data stored by the app stays on your device until you clear the app’s data or uninstall it. Data processed by Google is retained according to Google’s policies.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version, and the current version is always available in the app’s Settings.
