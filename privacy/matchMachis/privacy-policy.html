Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026 · Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy explains how the Math Machis app ("the app"), published by Balliram ("we", "us"), handles information. Math Machis is a matchstick puzzle game. It needs no account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. Your game progress stays on your device. The app uses Google services that process limited device data: Google AdMob to show ads, Firebase Cloud Messaging to send push notifications if you allow them, Cloud Firestore to download the app's ad settings, and Google Play to offer app updates. Optional daily reminders are scheduled and shown by your device itself; nothing about them is sent to us or to Google. We do not sell your personal data.

1. Data stored on your device

The app saves the following locally, in your device's app storage:

  • puzzle progress: stars, solved levels, your daily-challenge streak, daily goals progress and endless-mode best score;
  • your hint balance;
  • settings: sound on or off, English or Hindi, daily reminders on or off, and which version of this policy and the Terms of Use you agreed to.

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. Delete it any time with Settings → Delete game data, by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling the app.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Math Machis shows banner ads on menu screens, occasional full-screen ads between levels, and optional reward videos that give you extra hints. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve, personalise (where permitted) and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • your Advertising ID and other device identifiers (ad serving, frequency capping, measurement, and personalisation where you have allowed it);
  • your IP address, used for approximate location (ad serving and fraud prevention);
  • your interactions with ads, and diagnostics (measuring ad performance and keeping the SDK stable).

Google processes this data under its own terms: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing / Apple App Store)

Math Machis offers optional purchases such as hint packs and an ad-free option. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iOS). The app does not receive or store any payment information — card numbers, billing addresses and transaction processing stay with the store. The app only receives a purchase token and product ID so it can grant the purchased hints or unlock the ad-free mode. Purchase history is stored locally on your device to restore your purchases if you reinstall the app or switch devices while signed in to the same store account. You can view or manage your purchase history in your Google Play or Apple account settings. Refund requests are handled by the store, not by us.

4. Notifications

Daily reminders. If you allow notifications, the app shows up to five reminders a day (for example when the Daily Challenge is ready, how your daily goals are going, or which puzzle is next), and a few "come back" reminders if you haven't played for some days. They are scheduled and shown by your device, using the progress saved on it. No push service is used for them, and nothing about the reminders is sent to us, to Google or to anyone else. Turn them off any time in Settings → Daily reminders or in your device's notification settings.

Push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging). The app can also receive notifications such as new puzzles, reminders and news. On Android 13 and later, and on iOS, it asks for your permission first. If you allow them, Firebase Cloud Messaging (provided by Google) creates for this install:

  • an FCM registration token and a Firebase installation ID (random identifiers for this install, not linked to your name or an account), to deliver notifications;
  • device and app information (such as app version, operating system and language) and your IP address, to deliver notifications and keep the service reliable.

The app subscribes to a general topic so that announcements reach all players. We do not use notifications for third-party advertising. Turn notifications off any time in your device settings; this does not affect the game.

5. App configuration (Cloud Firestore)

When the app starts, it downloads its ad settings (which ad units to use, and whether ads are on) from Google Cloud Firestore. This is a read-only request: the app sends no game data, progress or personal information. As with any internet request, Google's servers receive your IP address and basic technical details to answer it. If the download fails, the app uses settings built into the app.

6. App updates (Google Play)

On Android, the app uses Google Play's in-app update service to check whether a newer version is available and, if so, to offer it. That check happens between your device and Google Play. We receive no personal data from it.

7. Sharing with third parties

We share data only with the service providers named in this policy (Google AdMob, Google Firebase and Google Play), and only for the purposes described. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with data brokers. We may disclose information if required by law. More: firebase.google.com/support/privacy and support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

8. Permissions the app uses

  • Internet and network state: loading ads, notifications, app settings and update checks.
  • Notifications (Android 13+ and iOS ask you first): showing daily reminders and push notifications; optional.
  • Run at startup: restoring the daily reminders that are already scheduled after your device restarts.
  • Vibration: used by notifications.
  • Advertising ID: used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK, as described above.

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, files or location.

9. Your choices

  • Ad consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): after you agree to these terms, the app shows Google's consent form. Change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Reward videos are always optional.
  • Notifications: allow or block them when asked, or later in your device settings.
  • Daily reminders: turn them off any time in Settings → Daily reminders.

10. Children

Math Machis is a general-audience puzzle game. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help get it removed.

11. Security and retention

All data the app sends is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). We do not run our own servers, and we do not keep a copy of your game data.

  • Game data stays on your device until you delete it, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
  • Notification identifiers are kept by Firebase while the app is installed. They stop working when you uninstall the app, and Firebase deletes inactive identifiers automatically (generally after about 270 days without use).
  • Advertising data is retained by Google according to its own policies.

12. Deleting your data

Because the app has no accounts, you can delete everything it stores with Settings → Delete game data, by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling it. To ask us to delete the notification identifiers linked to your install, email us at the address below; we will respond within 30 days. You can reset your Advertising ID in your device settings.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no account data, most requests are handled by deleting game data, turning off notifications, your device's ad settings, or by Google for advertising data. You can also contact us.

14. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it and its effective date, and the app will show you the new version and ask you to agree before you continue playing.
