Chilli Bus: Traffic Jam

📱 Overview

Clear the traffic jam and board passengers onto matching buses. 3D vehicles and the passenger crowd run in Unity; Flutter handles the HUD, coins, missions, boosters and ads.

✨ Key Features

3D bus puzzle

Unity board with shaped passenger crowds, boarding animations and win/lose logic.

Missions

Clear levels, dispatch buses and earn coins to complete missions.

Decorations & shop

Spend coins on decorations and boosters; free lucky spin.

Live ops

Firebase in-app messaging, FCM push, Firestore ad config and in-app updates.

🛠️ Technical Stack

Flutter Unity 3D Flame flutter_unity_widget Firebase AdMob
