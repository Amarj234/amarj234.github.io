Brain Top: Arrow Exit

📱 Overview

Tap a snake-like arrow to slide it off the board; if something is in its way it crashes and you lose a heart. Rendered with flutter_scene on Flutter GPU.

✨ Key Features

Endless generated levels

Every level is generated from its number, 97–99% filled and always solvable.

Flutter GPU rendering

Instanced meshes with flutter_scene for exit, crash, erase and hint animations.

Code-generated audio

All effects and the music loop are synthesised in code, with gapless looping.

Rewards

Lucky spin, daily rewards, hints and erasers earned in the game.

🛠️ Technical Stack

Flutter flutter_scene Flutter GPU vector_math Firebase AdMob
