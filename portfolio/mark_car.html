Mark Car: Draw Bridge

📱 Overview

Draw one line, watch it fall, and guide your car to the flag! 180 physics puzzles. Your drawing falls, tilts and settles with real physics. Too short and it tumbles. Too steep and the car flips. Just right - cruise to victory!

✨ Key Features

Draw Real Physics Bridges

Your line becomes a solid bridge with real physics. It falls, tilts, settles - plan carefully!

Ink Meter & 3-Star Rating

Use less ink for perfect 3-star bridges. Efficiency rewarded!

180 Hand-crafted & Procedural Levels

Gaps, cliffs, floating platforms, walls, deep canyons, islands, oncoming traffic, swinging crushers.

8 Beautiful Worlds

Sunny meadows, pine forests, tropical lagoons, golden deserts, sunset hills, dusk cities, night cities, gritty factory.

Daily Streak & Goals

Play a level daily to grow streak. Complete 3 daily goals for free hints. Optional reminders.

Hint & Skip System

Watch optional video for guide line, or skip a level you can't crack.
