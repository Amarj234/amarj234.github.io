Food Look: Yummy Runner Games

📱 Overview

Food Look packs FOUR tasty runner games into one app! 🍜🍦🍔🍓 Hungry for fun? Carry bowls through topping gates, stack the tallest ice cream tower, build burger towers, and transform fruits - all with simple one-finger controls.

✨ Key Features

4 Games in One

Ramen Rush, Scoop Stack, Burger Tower, Fruit Funner - each with unique food-themed mechanics and levels.

Gate-based Math Puzzles

Pick the best gates (+5, ×2, -5) to grow your score. Quick mental math while running!

8 Unlockable Characters

Mia, Leo, Chef Toni, Shadow, Captain Bo, Bella, Max and Zara - each with unique style.

Power-ups & Rewards

Fruit magnet, protective shield, optional rewarded ads for revives and coin multipliers.

Offline Play

Play anywhere - no internet needed for the core gameplay.

x10 Finish Bonus

Reach serving pads and finish stairs for up to 10x score multiplier!

🛠️ Technical Stack

Flutter Unity 3D AdMob Firebase IAP
