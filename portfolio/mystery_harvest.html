Mystery Harvest

📱 Overview

Ready for a brain-teasing harvest adventure? Mystery Harvest combines satisfying sorting mechanics with strategic puzzle-solving across 500 unique levels! Tap a lane to reveal its top fruit, tap another to move it there, match 4 to harvest.

✨ Key Features

500 Unique Levels

Progressive difficulty: Easy → Medium → Hard → Expert → Master. Hand-crafted puzzles.

6 Fruit Types

Strawberry, Eggplant, Blueberry, Grape, Tomato, Pepper - each with distinct visuals.

Smart Hint System

Stuck? Get a smart move suggestion. Undo mistakes, mix board for fresh perspective.

5 Beautiful Themes

Default, Dark, Autumn, Ocean, Sunset - customize your visual experience.

Offline-First Design

Play anywhere - no internet needed! Fair-play 10-min daily limit, unlimited with "Remove Ads".

Privacy First

No personal data collected. All data stored locally. No tracking, no analytics. COPPA compliant.

🛠️ Technical Stack

Flutter AdMob Local Storage IAP
