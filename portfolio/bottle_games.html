Bottle Games: Rush & Squash

📱 Overview

Three addictive, colorful games in one free app! 💧🙌🍉 Bottle Games packs three quick, satisfying hyper-casual games that are easy to pick up and hard to put down. Every level takes about a minute, perfect for short breaks.

✨ Key Features

Bottle Rush

Steer a walking water bottle down a sky track. Grab water drops, run through blue gates to fill up, dodge spikes and red gates. Reach 100% for the thirsty giant!

Long Arms Chase

Thief running for his boat! Collect hands to stretch arms longer, avoid cones and saws, catch the thief before escape.

Squeezy Girl

Hold to charge, release to jump! Leap pillar to pillar, land bottom-first on watermelons, bananas, tomatoes, water balloons. Perfect landing = PERFECT!

Daily Challenges

3 new challenges every day. Finish all to build streak. How many days in a row can you keep it?

Trophies & Stats

Unlock trophies as you play. Track best results in every game. Progress stays on device.

Offline Play

No account or sign-up needed. Plays offline (ads need connection). Small, fast, free.
