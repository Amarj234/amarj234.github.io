QuickShoot: Video Editor

📱 Overview

QuickShoot is a fast, professional video editor that runs entirely on the phone: no account, no watermark, and videos never leave the device.

✨ Key Features

Multi-track timeline

Video, photo, text, sticker and audio layers with trim, split, snap, keyframes and unlimited undo/redo.

Effects & transitions

GPU filters, LUT (.cube) import, chroma key, blend modes, speed ramps, reverse and freeze frame.

AI background removal

Cut people out of the background on-device and replace it with a colour, blur or image.

Native Media3 engine

Preview and export run on Android Media3 through a platform-channel bridge, with autosave and crash recovery.

🛠️ Technical Stack

Flutter Android Media3 Kotlin Platform Channels Riverpod Isolates AdMob
