Live IP Camera: Mobile Webcam

📱 Overview

Turn any Android phone into a live Wi-Fi IP camera in one tap. Live IP Camera streams your phone's camera to any web browser on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot. Open the link on your laptop, tablet, smart TV or another phone and watch live — no account, no cloud and no extra app needed.

✨ Key Features

MJPEG Streaming

Watch in Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, smart TVs and VLC. Front and back camera support.

Quality Settings

Low (240p), Medium (480p), High (720p) - adapt to your network speed.

Background Streaming

Keeps streaming with screen off. Stop any time from notification. Foreground service with CAMERA/MIC permissions.

Optional Audio

Share live audio from microphone (off by default). Toggle in settings.

Browser Recording

Record video with sound from browser — saved as MP4/WebM on your computer. Snapshot & fullscreen buttons.

Private by Design

Video streamed directly on local network. Never uploaded, recorded or stored by us. Works over Wi-Fi or hotspot.
