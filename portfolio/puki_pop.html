Puki Pop: Bubble Shooter

📱 Overview

Dive into a mesmerizing cosmic arcade experience where reflexes meet strategy. Orbs spiral around a hungry black hole — aim, fire, and pop them before they cross the event horizon! Two game modes in one.

✨ Key Features

Two Game Modes

Classic Mode (60 levels, orbit-shooting) and Marble Blast (40 levels, match-3 chain shooter).

Daily Goals & Rewards

3 fresh goals every day — complete them for stardust & boosters. Claim daily bonus when all three done.

Strategic Boosters

Nova Bomb (obliterates clusters), Prism Orb (matches any color), Time Freeze (stops orbit).

Star Progression

Collect up to 3 stars per level (100+ levels total). Unlock new levels by earning stars.

Offline Play

No internet required. Play anywhere - plane, subway, cabin.

Haptic Feedback

Satisfying vibration for every pop. Portrait-only, one-handed gameplay.
