Runner Reels: 3D Cartoon Maker

📱 Overview

Make cute 3D cartoon reels right on your phone, in one tap! Runner Reels creates a brand new short cartoon every time: chibi characters, a fun story, voices, music and sound effects. Save it to your gallery and share as a reel or short.

✨ Key Features

6 Story Templates

Rescue, Boss Fight, Thirsty Friend, Frozen Friend, Chase, Food Stack - each with unique 3D animations.

On-device AI Stories

Optional local LLM download (llama.cpp) writes new titles and dialogue for every reel, fully offline.

Quick Math Gates

"HELP ME!" math problems pop up during the run: 2 + 3 = ? Pick the right gate to continue!

Chibi Characters

Big-eyed characters in bunny, penguin, unicorn, dino, panda and more hoods react to every moment.

Privacy First

No account needed. Stories, voices and videos are made on your phone. Nothing leaves the device.

Optional Daily Reminder

Friendly nudge when a new story is ready. Turn off anytime in settings.

🛠️ Technical Stack

Flutter Unity llama.cpp On-device AI AdMob
