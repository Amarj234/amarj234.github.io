Danger Hills: Keep Defense

📱 Overview

A portrait canyon-defence game: raiders march up a winding desert road toward your keep. Steer the Warden, collect coins and build archer towers, a boulder ramp and upgrades. A 3D Unity scene is embedded in a Flutter app.

✨ Key Features

Animated hero

Rigged ranger with idle, jog, sprint, aim and shoot animations, auto-targeting and a coin magnet.

Build & upgrade

Archer towers, a boulder ramp, Warden training and keep repairs unlock in a simple chain.

60-level campaign

Three maps (midday, sunset, night) and a town that grows from hamlet to city as you progress.

Two control modes

Joystick or tap-to-move, saved between sessions.

🛠️ Technical Stack

Flutter Unity 3D flutter_unity_widget C# Firebase AdMob
