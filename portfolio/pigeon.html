  • 📖 Overview

    🔹 Pigeon Chat is a secure communication platform for businesses and organizations. It provides end-to-end encrypted messaging, voice, and video calls to protect sensitive information — combining business-grade security with admin-controlled access for fast, reliable, and private professional collaboration.
     

    ✨ Key Features

    ✅ End-to-end encrypted chats and calls
    ✅ Secure voice & video calling
    ✅ Business-grade security with admin-controlled access
    ✅ Simple, fast & reliable collaboration
    ✅ Available on Web, Android & iOS
     

    💻 Tech Stack & More

    ✅ Flutter & Dart (Android, iOS & Web)
    ✅ Real-time messaging over Sockets
    ✅ End-to-end encryption
    ✅ WebRTC voice & video calling
    ✅ Push Notifications
    ✅ Clean Architecture with BLoC state management
