DX War: Squad Shooter

📱 Overview

DX War is a squad shooter: your squad auto-fires up a bridge while a huge horde marches toward it. The Flutter app runs menus, cards, league and HUD, and embeds a Unity battle through flutter_unity_widget.

✨ Key Features

Huge crowds

Instanced rendering with baked run-cycle mesh frames keeps hundreds of soldiers smooth on phones.

Gates, heroes & bosses

Shoot gates to multiply your squad, recruit mech heroes, upgrade guns and beat boss levels.

Meta game

Collectible cards, booster packs, Champions League, base invasion, a 30-tier season pass and daily quests.

Flutter ↔ Unity bridge

JSON message protocol between the Flutter UI and the Unity GameManager.

🛠️ Technical Stack

Flutter Unity 2022.3 flutter_unity_widget C# Firebase AdMob
