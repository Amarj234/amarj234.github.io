FlingShot: Knock Down Blocks

📱 Overview

Aim, fire and clear the board. Flutter draws the UI while Unity renders a photoreal "tabletop toy world" and runs the physics.

✨ Key Features

Colour-match physics

Matching cannonballs pop chains of same-colour blocks; everything else gets knocked by real physics.

PBR rendering

HDR, soft shadows, reflection probes, bloom, ACES tone mapping and depth of field.

Adaptive quality

Measures fps and steps effects down so budget phones hold 60 fps.

Hard mode

A separate precision progression with target colours and solver-checked fairness.
