Multiplex Car: Drive & Park

📱 Overview

Get behind the wheel in Multiplex Car - three driving games in one! Park on sky-high decks, beat stunt courses, and evolve your ride through 7 tiers from Rust Bucket to Photon X. 90 handcrafted levels, 6 unlockable cars, 12 paint colors.

✨ Key Features

Precision Park

Steer along narrow elevated sky roads, weave past cones and crossing traffic, slot into glowing bay on multi-storey deck. Every scratch costs a star!

Stunt Trials

Swinging hammers, sweepers, pistons, seesaws, wrecking balls, narrow beams, huge jump gaps. Hit checkpoints, reach finish.

Evolve Rush

Steer through EVO gates to upgrade through 7 tiers - Rust Bucket to Photon X. Gold x2 gates, final ramp onto x1-x10 multiplier zones!

Garage & Customization

6 unlockable cars and 12 paint colors. Steering wheel or button controls, nitro boost and drift.

270 Stars to Earn

90 levels across 3 modes, 3 stars each. Daily coin bonus. Graphics, sound and vibration settings.

Offline Play

Plays offline - progress saved on your device. No internet required.

🛠️ Technical Stack

Flutter AdMob Firebase Local Storage
